# Hospital Management System (HMS)

## Project Overview
The Hospital Management System (HMS) is designed to simplify and streamline hospital operations through a centralized digital platform. It aims to improve operational efficiency, patient care, and administrative management.

## Project Objectives
- Streamline hospital operations.
- Improve patient registration and management.
- Simplify appointment scheduling.
- Manage billing and payments.
- Improve coordination between hospital departments.

## Functional Modules
1. Patient Management
2. Doctor Management
3. Appointment Management
4. OPD Management
5. IPD Management
6. Billing and Payments
7. Pharmacy Management
8. Laboratory Management
9. Housekeeping Management (Optional)
10. HR and Payroll Management (Optional)

## User Roles
- Super Admin
- Hospital Administrator
- Doctor
- Receptionist
- Nurse
- Pharmacist
- Lab Technician
- Accountant
